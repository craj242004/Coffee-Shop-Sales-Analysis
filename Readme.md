# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data to understand revenue performance, order volume, product demand, sales trends, store performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

The project combines **MySQL SQL analysis** with an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to transform transaction-level sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard allows users to analyze sales performance by month, day, weekday/weekend, store location, product category, product type, and hour of the day.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze coffee shop sales data and answer important business questions such as:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- How many orders and products are sold?
- How does sales performance change over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which individual products perform best?
- Which store locations generate the highest sales?
- How do weekday and weekend sales compare?
- Which hours have the highest sales activity?
- Which days perform above or below the average daily sales?

The analysis provides a data-driven view of sales performance that can help identify important sales patterns and areas for further investigation.

## 🔍 Data Preparation & SQL Analysis

The raw transaction data required several transformations before analysis.

### Data preparation performed using SQL

- Converted `transaction_date` from text into a proper `DATE` data type.
- Converted `transaction_time` into a proper `TIME` data type.
- Renamed the incorrectly encoded transaction ID column to `transaction_id`.
- Prepared the dataset for time-based analysis.

### SQL concepts used

- `SUM()`
- `COUNT()`
- `ROUND()`
- `AVG()`
- `GROUP BY`
- `ORDER BY`
- `WHERE`
- `CASE`
- `MONTH()`
- `DAY()`
- `DAYOFWEEK()`
- `HOUR()`
- `LAG()`
- Window Functions
- Subqueries

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis, several important patterns can be observed.

### Overall Sales Performance

For the February 2023 dashboard view:

- Total sales were approximately **$76K**.
- The business recorded approximately **16.4K orders**.
- Approximately **23.6K items** were sold.

### Weekday vs Weekend

- Weekdays generated approximately **$54K**, representing about **71%** of sales.
- Weekends generated approximately **$22K**, representing about **29%** of sales.
- This indicates that the selected period had substantially higher sales activity during weekdays.

### Product Category Performance

- **Coffee** was the highest-revenue product category at approximately **$29.27K**.
- **Tea** followed with approximately **$21.73K**.
- Bakery generated approximately **$9.04K**.
- Drinking Chocolate generated approximately **$8.13K**.

This shows that coffee and tea were major contributors to overall sales in the selected period.

### Top Product Performance

Among the products displayed:

- **Barista Espresso** generated approximately **$10.03K**.
- **Brewed Chai Tea** generated approximately **$8.38K**.
- **Hot Chocolate** generated approximately **$8.13K**.
- **Gourmet brewed coffee** generated approximately **$7.65K**.

### Store Performance

The three displayed store locations had relatively close sales performance:

- Hell's Kitchen: approximately **$25.72K**
- Lower Manhattan: approximately **$25.32K**
- Astoria: approximately **$25.11K**

The relatively small difference between locations indicates that sales were distributed fairly evenly across the three stores during the selected period.

### Daily Sales

The dashboard compares daily sales against the average daily sales benchmark, making it possible to identify days performing above or below the period average.

### Hourly Sales

The hourly/day heatmap provides a view of sales activity throughout the day and helps identify periods with higher and lower sales volume.
